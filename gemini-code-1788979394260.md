# Macbease — Comprehensive Product & Feature Audit

## 1. App Identity & Core Value Proposition

* **App Name & Identity**: **Macbease** (internal routing domain: `https://app.macbease.com/app/profi...`). It is visually identified by a Gothic **"S"** monogram logo and structured around an astronomical exploration motif ("Universes" for institutes, "Territories" for campus regions, "Cosmic Reader" mascots, and "Interstellar Points" for rewards).
* **Primary Purpose**: An all-in-one campus social network, community operating system, and digital student life hub. It consolidates campus club administration, academic forums, real-time 2D/3D territory heatmaps, short-form video reels ("Snippets"), event ticketing with tier-locked chat rooms, calendar-indexed memory tracking, and student loyalty rewards.
* **Target Audience**: College and university students, student-led organizations, collegiate event organizers, and campus content creators (demonstrated across institutes such as Lovely Professional University and IIT Bombay).

## 2. Feature-by-Feature Breakdown

### A. Geospatial Mapping & Campus "Territories"
* **Macro Multiverse Map**: A pan-India interactive map view displaying university clusters nationwide with geographic nodes and institution activity rankings.
* **Micro Campus Heatmap & Territories**: Zoomed-in topological map of a campus (e.g., LPU) divided into dynamic functional zones ("Territories").
* **Domain Polygons & Student Density**: Color-coded map areas representing academic and extracurricular interest groups with live member callout counts (e.g., *Venture Mindset* - 188 members, *Tech Innovators* - 232 members, *Celestial Frontiers* - 144 members, *Code Craft*, *Rhythmic Expression*).
* **Corporate & Placement Nodes**: Territory markers highlighting corporate interest clusters and placement networks (e.g., *TCS*, *Capgemini*, *Blinkit*, *Infosys Alumni Network*, *Macbease*).
* **Perspective Controls**: Includes a `2D` / `3D` perspective switcher, a GPS re-center icon, route tracing tools, and a draggable bottom sheet indexing all campus territories.

### B. Event Marketplace & QR Ticketing
* **Events Marketplace**: Search and register for campus competitions, hackathons, workshops, and fests (e.g., *Word War*, *Code2Career AI Hackathon*, *Code Heist*, *Developers Meet*, *Anime Night 2.0*, *Paper Mache Workshop*).
* **Tiered Pricing & Registration**: Displays price tags (`Free`, `₹60/-`, `₹99 onwards`, `₹179 onwards`, `₹199 onwards`) paired with direct `Book now!` or `Buy Tickets` CTAs.
* **"My Tickets" Passbook**: Digital pass wallet styled as perforated ticket stubs containing event organizers (*DSO*, *Play2Unite*), event venues (*Unipolis*, *Shanti Devi Mittal Auditorium*), booking IDs, and scannable QR matrices.
* **Status Lifecycle**: Color-coded tags distinguishing `Active` (upcoming) from `Redeemed` (attended) passes, automatically enabling a post-event review prompt (`Review the event.`).
* **Tier-Gated Event Chat Rooms**: Segmented group chats populated directly by ticket purchases (e.g., *Comic Verse* separates users into a public `General Chat` with 378 members and a ticket-locked `silver Chat` with 180 members).

### C. Clubs, Academic Communities & Gamified Leaderboards
* **Extracurricular Clubs Directory**: A searchable registry of verified student clubs (e.g., *Coding Club*, *Pawn Knight Chess*, *Designer's Paradise*, *Sheyn*). Cards feature founder credits, categorization hashtags, member counters, and user star ratings (e.g., `☆ 6777 rating`, `487 members`).
* **Academic Communities**: Course-specific study channels (e.g., *Operating_Systems / CSE 316*, *Problematic Soils / SOL 220*, *Penetration_Testing / CSC 304*) for sharing class notes and technical resources.
* **Filter Pills**: Rapid sorting across `All`, `Leader Board`, `Academics`, `Sports`, and `Cultural`.
* **Interstellar Points Leaderboard**: Live ranking widget displaying top clubs on trophy podiums (Gold, Silver, Bronze) based on accumulated points (e.g., *Cricket Passionates* - 1278 pts, *got-it!* - 997 pts, *Photography* - 964 pts).

### D. Social Feed, Short Video "Snippets" & Publishing
* **Campus Story Highlights**: Circular status rings at the top of the feed (e.g., *0x0CAFE*, *Social Club*) with a `+` create trigger.
* **Campus Activity Feed**: Feed cards supporting rich text, roadmaps, cafe location tags (*Suto Cafe, Unimall*), timestamps, likes, comments, and bookmarking.
* **Short-Form Video ("Snippets for you")**: Vertical 9:16 video reel feed showcasing student talent, performances, and micro-content.
* **Speed-Dial Creation Menu**: Floating action button (`+ Create`) that expands into dual options:
  1. `Create Memory` (Camera glyph: photo/video capture).
  2. `Add Post` (Quill glyph: rich-text discussion posts).
* **Interactive Comment Drawer**: Threaded discussions displaying verified student badges (*Lovely Professional University*), relative timestamps, and a quick-reaction emoji bar.

### E. Student Profile, Memory Lane & Personal Expression
* **Profile Metrics & Completion Meter**: Displays user handle (`@avenue017`), institution affiliation, follower counters, and a radial setup progress meter (`33% Completed`).
* **Verification Trust Badges**: Official badges for `ID Verification` (student ID) and `Phone Verification` (contact-based friend discovery).
* **Academic & Personal Info**: Custom fields for Stream, Course Name, Degree Level (`UG`, `PG`, `PhD`), Pronouns, and customizable Interest Tags.
* **Memory Lane Calendar**: Monthly calendar grid (e.g., September 2026) that catalogs campus memories date-by-date.
* **Memory Management & Trash Bin**: Dedicated storage drawers for `Memory list (0)` (people tagged in memories), `Bookmarks (0)`, and `Memory Bin (0)` (trash/recycle bin for deleted memories).
* **Profile Expression Shelves**: Quick-action hubs to `Add Books`, `Add Letter`, `Add Movies`, and `Express Yourself`.
* **Animated Mascot Customizer ("Stack Books")**: Visual avatar customizer offering illustrated animated characters (`Cosmic Reader`, `Book Worm`, `Introvert Girl`, `Reading Wizard`).
* **Deep Linking & QR Sharing**: Generates a permanent URL (`https://app.macbease.com/app/profi...`) and a scannable 2D QR matrix for in-person networking.

### F. Rewards, Quests & Account Security
* **Interstellar Points Engine**: Centralized point tracker (` 0`) that logs engagement currency.
* **Special Offers ("Fresh drops")**: In-app partner marketplace for student discounts and redeemable rewards.
* **Interstellar Quests ("Mission control")**: Challenge-based campus quests where students complete tasks to earn points.
* **Account Deactivation Policy**: Two-step account termination protocol featuring a 29-day recovery grace period before permanent data deletion.
* **Moderation & Blocking**: Blocked users list with messaging guardrails (blocked accounts cannot send text messages or asynchronous digital letters).
* **Theme Customizer**: Quick-switch segmented pill for toggling between `DAY MODE` and Night Mode.
